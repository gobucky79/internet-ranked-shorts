# Insights (read before picking topics)

## What works for us
- 2026-10-08: "These Everyday Things Are Secretly Tricking You" (list format) held 69.9% avg viewed; "The Arrow on Your Gas Gauge
  Has a Secret" (single fact stretched to ~54s) held 56%. Lists/rankings beat single facts; single facts should be short.

- 2026-10-10 (MrBeast advice, from a compilation the creator shared): give viewers what they clicked for right away and stay on
  topic; quality beats upload volume (average videos never get pushed, so ship fewer but great); thumbnails must be simple,
  instantly understood, trigger one emotion and real curiosity.

- 2026-10-10 niche research: most Shorts views go to entertainment (~17–32%), gaming (~21%), tutorials/hacks (~18%);
  all-time top Shorts are food, transformations, skits, stunts, DIY. Facts/"did you know" grows fast but is crowded with
  modest RPM. → Daily topics rotate through the Top 5 (README), keeping our ranking format.
