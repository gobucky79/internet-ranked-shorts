About the Internet Ranked Bot

The Internet Ranked Bot is a private tool used only by the channel owner. It edits the day's compilation, uploads it to the Internet Ranked YouTube channel as a private video for review, saves a copy to the owner's Google Drive, and reads the channel's own view statistics for a weekly report. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
